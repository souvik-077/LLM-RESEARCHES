# My LLM Research Journey

## Background

Before starting this journey, I knew nothing about LLMs beyond having heard 
the term. During JAVA Lab, Dinesh Sir introduced how ChatGPT works and 
explained how AI models function. This led to a discussion on 
**Hallucination** — a major problem faced by LLMs.

That curiosity led to a broader conversation about real-world applications 
and the future scope of this field, along with open problems worth solving. 
Sir shared resources on Hallucination research, then introduced me to 
**Knowledge Graphs (KG)**, **Multi-modal Knowledge Graphs (MKG)**, 
**GraphRAG**, **KG-Adapters**, and **Multi-Hop Reasoning** — and continued 
guiding me until I understood the basic ideas.

**Task given:** Understand Multi-Hop Reasoning.

### Methodology
The paper I studied follows a survey-based methodology, analyzing recent 
developments in Knowledge Graph-Augmented Generation (Graph-RAG). Rather 
than proposing a new Graph-RAG model, it systematically reviews existing 
research and organizes the techniques into a unified framework.

### Conclusion
Graph-RAG is a significant advancement over traditional text-based 
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for building trustworthy LLMs.
